# Materials-Science Research Guide

Use this guide when the topic includes Al-Sc, Al-Sc-Zr, Al3Sc or Al3(Sc,Zr), precipitation strengthening, recrystallization, grain refinement, microstructure, TEM, SEM, EBSD, or mechanical properties.

## Preserve experimental context

Mechanism and property claims are interpretable only with adequate context. Capture where available:

- full alloy composition and impurity limits;
- casting, homogenization, deformation, solution treatment, aging, heating rate, and cooling history;
- specimen location, orientation, thickness, and prior grain/subgrain state;
- precipitate composition, crystal structure, size distribution, number density, volume fraction, morphology, and coherency evidence;
- recrystallized fraction, grain size definition, texture, boundary criteria, and spatial sampling;
- TEM acquisition/analysis, SEM conditions, EBSD step size and cleanup rules, and complementary characterization;
- hardness, tensile or other property-test standard, direction, strain rate, temperature, sample count, scatter, and uncertainty.

Do not compare values across papers without checking these conditions.

## Mechanism evidence map

For claims involving Al3Sc or Al3(Sc,Zr) and recrystallization, separate:

1. direct observations of precipitate state;
2. direct observations of recovery/recrystallization or boundary motion;
3. temporal and spatial correspondence between the two;
4. model-based explanations such as pinning, solute effects, or strengthening;
5. competing effects of composition, deformation, dispersoid coarsening, texture, and thermal history;
6. property consequences and whether they were measured or inferred.

Label a mechanism `supported` only when the evidence links the proposed cause to the observed response under the relevant condition. Correlation, post-treatment snapshots, or a review's schematic alone may support an interpretation but not a definitive causal claim.

## Search axes

Combine topic terms across these axes rather than relying on one query:

- material: `Al-Sc`, `Al-Sc-Zr`, `aluminium scandium zirconium`, `Al3Sc`, `Al3(Sc,Zr)`, dispersoid;
- process: homogenization, aging, annealing, deformation, additive manufacturing, casting, rolling, extrusion;
- mechanism: precipitation, coarsening, nucleation, Zener pinning, recovery, recrystallization, grain boundary, grain refinement;
- characterization: TEM, STEM, SEM, EBSD, diffraction, atom probe, calorimetry;
- outcome: strength, hardness, ductility, thermal stability, recrystallized fraction, grain size, texture.

Record variant spellings and chemical notation used in each database.

## Research-gap checks

Before claiming a gap, test whether apparent disagreement is explained by:

- different Sc/Zr content or impurity background;
- different thermomechanical paths;
- as-cast versus wrought or additively manufactured material;
- precipitate-resolution limits or inconsistent size metrics;
- two-dimensional versus three-dimensional characterization;
- local microscopy versus bulk property inference;
- short-term isothermal treatment versus continuous heating or long exposure;
- different recrystallization definitions, EBSD thresholds, or sampling strategies.

A strong gap names the untested condition or missing causal link and identifies the experiment or cross-scale evidence needed to resolve it.
